# ☭ Values

unifi-map is a socialist free-software project. It exists to put useful
technical means in the hands of the people who use them, rather than create
private leverage over them.

The hammer and sickle marks this commitment to worker ownership, solidarity,
and the public good. It is not a condition of use or contribution.

## What that means here

- The project is AGPL-3.0-only. People can use, study, modify, and share it;
  modified versions distributed to others, including through a network service,
  must make their corresponding source available under the same license.
- There will be no proprietary edition, feature-gated "open core", mandatory
  account, telemetry business model, or private sponsor channel that controls
  the public roadmap.
- The project is local-first. It does not require a cloud relay or send network
  data to a service operated by this project.
- Everyone may use or contribute to the project. No political identity,
  affiliation, or vocabulary is required.

## Governance

Today, the project has one maintainer. If a sustained, good-faith contributor
collective forms, governance should become collective and public: decisions,
their rationale, and the people making them should be visible to the community.

Participation in governance requires commitment to these values in practice.
That includes rejecting attempts to privatize, capture, or exploit the project,
and treating other participants with respect. Disagreement about technical
design or politics is welcome; harassment, discrimination, sabotage, and
bad-faith obstruction are not.

This is an intention to build real collective governance when there is a
collective to govern with, not a promise of a paper bureaucracy in advance.
